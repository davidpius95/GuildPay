Help & Support

🔍
Account & Security
How do I verify my account?▼
Go to Profile > KYC Verification. Complete the 3-step process: personal info, address, and identity document. Sandbox accounts are auto-approved. Production accounts are reviewed within 24 hours.
How do I reset my PIN?▼
Go to Profile > Security & PIN. You'll need your current PIN to set a new one. If you've forgotten your PIN, contact support for a reset.
What are KYC tiers?▼
Tier 0: Unverified ($500/day, $2,000/month). Tier 1: Basic KYC ($5,000/day, $25,000/month). Tier 2: Full KYC ($25,000/day, $100,000/month). Upgrade by completing identity verification.
Transfers & Payments
How long do transfers take?▼
Gulf to Africa: 80% settle within 15 minutes via our Nium + Ecobank partnership. Local transfers (within Nigeria/Ghana): typically instant. SWIFT transfers: 1-3 business days.
What are the transfer fees?▼
Fees vary by corridor and amount. We show the exact fee before you confirm any transfer. There are no hidden charges — the rate you see is the rate you get.
Which countries can I send to?▼
We support 35+ countries across Africa and the Gulf. Major corridors include Qatar/UAE to Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and South Africa. Check the Send flow for the full list.
Virtual Cards
How do virtual cards work?▼
Virtual cards let you shop online anywhere Visa/Mastercard is accepted. Fund your card from your wallet, and spend in USD, NGN, or other supported currencies. You can freeze/unfreeze your card anytime.
Can I use my card for subscriptions?▼
Yes! Virtual cards work for recurring payments like Netflix, Spotify, and other online subscriptions. Make sure your card is funded with enough balance to cover the renewal.
Bill Payments
What bills can I pay?▼
Airtime (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile, Safaricom), data bundles, electricity (Eko, Ikeja), TV cable (DSTV, GoTV, StarTimes), and internet services across Nigeria, Ghana, and Kenya.
